# Governance and project responsibility

OHPIPE is intended to process material within projects whose applicable requirements have already been addressed. Before operational use, the controller or joint controllers determine the permitted purpose, material, access, environment and outputs, with ethics review where required and advice from the data protection officer as appropriate.

The DPO advises and monitors. The software does not appoint the DPO as the authorizing decision-maker, perform legal assessment or grant ethics approval. Inclusion of a profile or model name is not permission to process another project's material.

Project teams configure access, retention, deletion, backups, keys, model services and operator roles. A local command can call a separately configured service; local execution alone does not prove isolation. Do not infer that every deployment is offline or every output safe to share.

The pipeline records workflow evidence. A decision reference does not establish the validity of its external legal or institutional basis. Journal authentication does not certify a reviewer's identity or competence.

Pseudonymisation is project-specific. Indirect identifiers, unmarked material and contextual relationships can remain identifying. The manual workflow depends on human markings, entity decisions, transformation rules and review of the resulting text. Synthetic tests cannot measure how completely real identifying information has been found.

Research teams remain responsible for source criticism, analytical choices and interpretations. Suggestions are proposals. Coverage and successful execution do not establish analytical quality.

The pilot endpoint is one transcript through reviewed descriptive proposals, completed on 13 September 2026. It does not establish general deployment suitability or export readiness. See [Testing](TESTING.md) for separate software-test evidence and its limits.
